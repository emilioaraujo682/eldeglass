Why we exist

Staying home shouldn't be the only safe choice.

Many older adults stop going out because the outside world got harder to read: small print on bus schedules, crowded stores, confusing intersections, and apps built for young thumbs. Little by little, home becomes the only place that feels easy.

EldeGlass builds glasses that look like regular glasses and work like a patient friend walking beside you. You just talk to them. They see what you see, and they answer out loud in a calm, clear voice.

Company summary

About EldeGlass

EldeGlass is a technology company that develops AI-powered smart glasses specifically designed for elderly people. The glasses contain an embedded AI assistant that analyzes the user's surroundings in real time and provides useful guidance through audio and visual assistance.

The glasses help users navigate streets, locate products while grocery shopping, understand signs, identify public transportation, receive reminders, and ask questions about their surroundings. For example, a user can ask the glasses where a certain product is located, which bus they should take, or how to reach a destination.

What makes EldeGlass unique is that it combines several forms of assistance into one simple wearable device. Instead of requiring elderly users to constantly operate a smartphone, the glasses let them receive assistance while naturally interacting with the world around them. The main benefit is greater independence, confidence, and mobility for elderly people who might otherwise depend heavily on relatives or caregivers.

Vision

A world where aging does not prevent people from living independently, confidently, and actively.

Mission

EldeGlass uses accessible artificial intelligence to help elderly people safely navigate everyday life, giving them the support they need to maintain their independence and participate more fully in the world around them.

Our goals and objectives

EldeGlass's main goal is to help elderly people regain independence and confidence in their everyday lives. We aim to make activities such as shopping, navigating cities, using public transportation, and completing everyday tasks safer and easier through artificial intelligence.

Another objective is to create technology that is simple enough for elderly users while remaining reliable, affordable, and useful in real-world situations.

Why the San Francisco Bay Area

The Bay Area offers access to highly skilled AI engineers, software developers, hardware designers, healthcare technology specialists, investors, and startup resources. This makes it easier to develop and improve a complex AI-powered wearable product.

Where EldeGlass is made

Our factories

We build every pair in our own facilities so we can control quality from the first lens to the final fitting. Our plants run on a mix of solar and grid power and hire locally.

Plant 1 · Villa Nueva, Guatemala

Assembly & Testing Center

Our main plant, where frames, cameras, speakers and batteries come together.

  • Up to 2,000 pairs assembled per day
  • Every pair passes a 40-point check, including a real walk-test outside
  • 40% of power comes from rooftop solar
Plant 2 · Heredia, Costa Rica

Lens & Optics Lab

Where we cut prescription lenses and add the tiny display layer that shows text on the glass.

  • Lenses cut to each customer's prescription
  • Clean-room coating line for the display layer
  • Partnership with local universities for optics training
Plant 3 · Mérida, Mexico

Repair & Recycling Center

Old pairs come back here to be repaired, refurbished and donated to senior centers, or recycled when they reach the end of their life. Our vans also deliver repaired glasses to customers' doors within 5 days.
