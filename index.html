Why we exist

Staying home shouldn't be the only safe choice.

Many older adults stop going out because the outside world got harder to read: small print on bus schedules, crowded stores, confusing intersections, and apps built for young thumbs. Little by little, home becomes the only place that feels easy.

Eldeglass builds glasses that look like regular glasses and work like a patient friend walking beside you. You just talk to them. They see what you see, and they answer out loud in a calm, clear voice.

What we do

Three trips, one pair of glasses

Eldeglass is built around the errands that keep people independent. Here is what a normal Tuesday looks like for a wearer.

Riding public transport

Eldeglass reads the bus number before it stops, counts down arrival times, and tells you which stop to get off at. A gentle tap on your temple when it's time to ring the bell.

Bus 12 is here. Your stop is the 6th one.

Grocery shopping

Say your list out loud at home. In the store, Eldeglass points you to the right aisle, reads tiny labels and expiry dates, and adds up your total before you reach the cashier.

The oats are on your left, second shelf.

Getting directions

Turn-by-turn guidance spoken in landmarks, not compass points. Eldeglass waits for the green light with you and warns about steps, curbs and uneven sidewalks.

Cross at the white stripes. The pharmacy is the green door.

Company summary

About Eldeglass

Eldeglass was founded in 2023 in Guatemala City by a small team of engineers, an optometrist and a social worker. The idea started with a grandmother who stopped taking the bus after she got lost twice. Her grandson, one of our founders, wanted to build something that would give her back her Tuesday market trips.

Today Eldeglass designs, manufactures and sells AI assistant glasses for adults over 65. We sell through opticians, pharmacies and senior centers, and we offer a monthly plan so families can share the cost. Every pair comes with in-person training at a local partner store, because nobody should have to learn new technology alone.

Our values

What we stand for

Independence first

We measure success by how often our wearers go out on their own, not by how long they look at a screen.

Patience and respect

Our assistant never rushes, never talks down and repeats itself as many times as you need. Older adults are experts in their own lives.

Privacy you can trust

Images are processed on the glasses and deleted right away. A light on the frame always shows when the camera is on.

Simple by design

Big text, few buttons, plain words. If a feature needs a manual to explain it, we redesign the feature.

Family in the loop

Loved ones can help with setup and get peace of mind, while the wearer stays in charge of what gets shared.

Made to last

Replaceable batteries, repairable frames and recycled acetate. A good pair of glasses should be with you for years.

Where Eldeglass is made

Our factories

We build every pair in our own facilities so we can control quality from the first lens to the final fitting. Our plants run on a mix of solar and grid power and hire locally.

Plant 1 · Villa Nueva, Guatemala

Assembly & Testing Center

Our main plant, where frames, cameras, speakers and batteries come together.

  • Up to 2,000 pairs assembled per day
  • Every pair passes a 40-point check, including a real walk-test outside
  • 40% of power comes from rooftop solar
Plant 2 · Heredia, Costa Rica

Lens & Optics Lab

Where we cut prescription lenses and add the tiny display layer that shows text on the glass.

  • Lenses cut to each customer's prescription
  • Clean-room coating line for the display layer
  • Partnership with local universities for optics training
Plant 3 · Mérida, Mexico

Repair & Recycling Center

Old pairs come back here to be repaired, refurbished and donated to senior centers, or recycled when they reach the end of their life. Our vans also deliver repaired glasses to customers' doors within 5 days.
